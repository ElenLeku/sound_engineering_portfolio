My Portfolio

i am a sound engineer looking to show some of my projects to the world, welcome!

i have studied this career for approximately three years in two different countries. working with talented artists has provided me with experience operating many different types of consoles and professional equipment and has helped me develop my knowledge in different genres of music.

through the years i learned the technicalities of this field and fine-tuned my ears to produce the best sounding music i can. in college i got to work not only with music, but also with animations, creating foley audio, and working on set for films.

it's fit and extremely important to mention that these songs are not to be distributed or monetized in any way, shape, or form. my sole purpose is to show my work. the artists who performed and wrote these songs are the rightful owners, and these form part of their intellectual property.
