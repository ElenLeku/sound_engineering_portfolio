Masters
this are songs that i have either mixed and mastered or only mastered. i don't have as much experience mastering as i do in recording or mixing but it's a skill i'm willing to work on and improve.

Photograph the Sun by Eliza Blue

eliza blue is a group of four very talented people who perform experimental, modern world-music. their complex creations include slow melodic music but also rhythmic, high-tempo beats.

i worked with eliza blue on my last semester of college and used this song as my final production. the recording was done in a neve custom 75 console, some instruments like the guitars, keys and vocals were recorded in studio, but the other elements of this song were midi notes given by the artist, i used logic pro X to change the sounds and make them my own, so i, in part, produced this song too. it's mixed and mastered by myslef on protools.

Call 911 by Kollinz

kollinz is an afro-beats artist that moved to berlin from ukraine and uses music to express his point of views and his feelings.
