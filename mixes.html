Recordings and Mixes
these songs i recorded and mixed as assingments for college. working in studio is my favourite part of audio, as you get to socialize with interesting and talented people. i was lucky to have worked with great artists that tought me a lot about thier sound and were patient with me while i was just starting my career and still had lots to learn.

Eternamente by Willy Mery

willy mery and company are a group of seven chileans that play traditional folk music. with heartwarming lyrics and a great rhythmic beat, they produce danceable and good-vibe music.

this session was recorded in an audient asp4816 16-channel, in-line console. several microphones were used to capture the group, some include the umt70s, u87, kms141 and a few others.

Love Again by Mersey Skys

this song was unfortunately never finished as the band split up. i mixed it as is and fixed the drums on logic pro X, using drum triggers to replace the recorded sound, which was done by someone else.

this songs lyrics are greately emotional and expresses truly how an artist can transmit his feelings through song.
