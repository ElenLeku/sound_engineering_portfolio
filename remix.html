Remix

The King of the Fairies

i remixed this song as part of an assignment on my last semester of university. i chose this irish fiddle sample that dates back a few hundred years. this melody embodies what ireland represents for me, nature and calmness. i created this whole project on logic pro X and a small midi keyboard. i took samples from the internet, used digital instruments and the built in plug-ins from logic to create, mix and master the song. i will in the near future re-record the violin sample.
